Resource center

The questions that decide it.

What's the difference between dual pricing and surcharging?

Dual pricing posts two prices on the same item — one for cash, one for card — so the customer chooses before they get to the register. Surcharging shows one price and adds a fee to card transactions at checkout. Surcharging carries card brand registration requirements and is restricted in a few states; dual pricing is generally simpler to run. Your rep will tell you which one fits your state and your average ticket.

Is the terminal really free?

Yes — terminal placement is included with your merchant account, with no upfront cost and no equipment lease. If you close the account, you send the hardware back. Full POS systems for salon, restaurant, and retail are quoted separately depending on the number of stations you need.

When does my money arrive?

Batches closed before the evening cutoff fund the next business day. Same-day funding is available on qualifying accounts, and we'll quote the flat per-batch fee before you enroll.

Am I locked into a contract?

No long-term contract, no annual fee, and no cancellation fee. If you leave, the account closes at the end of the billing month and the equipment goes back in the prepaid box.

Do I have to replace my current POS?

Often not. We integrate with most major POS platforms and can frequently re-point your existing system to our gateway. Send us the make and version and we'll confirm before you apply — if it won't work, we say so up front.

Do you speak my language?

Our support and sales teams serve merchants in English, Vietnamese, Spanish, and Korean across our five regional offices. Tell us your preference when you call and we'll route you to a rep who speaks it.
